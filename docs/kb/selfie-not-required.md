## What's happening

The policy setting to require a selfie at clock-in and clock-out saves, but
isn't enforced yet. A photo is only demanded when someone clocks in off-site,
or clocks out without a location.

## What you can do

For now there's no way to force a selfie on every clock-in. Off-site clocks
still need a remark and a photo, so those are covered.

## When to contact support

If your company must have a selfie on every clock-in for audit or compliance
reasons, so the need can be prioritised.
