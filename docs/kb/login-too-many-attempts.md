## What's happening

To protect accounts, AltomateHR limits how many sign-in attempts can be made
in a short time.

## What you can do

Wait about a minute, then try again. If you've forgotten your password, use
**Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page rather than retrying.

## When to contact support

If you're still refused after waiting, or you didn't make the attempts
yourself.
