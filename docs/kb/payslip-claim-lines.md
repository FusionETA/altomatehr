## What's happening

When an approved claim is paid through payroll, it's added to that month's pay
and shown on your payslip by name, so you can see exactly which claim was
reimbursed. That's why gross pay can be higher than your salary.

## What you can do

Nothing needs doing. Claims reimbursed this way aren't treated as income, so
they don't add to your tax.

## When to contact support

If a claim appears that you didn't make, or an approved claim is missing.
